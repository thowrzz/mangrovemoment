# 🪵 Coracle Ride

About This Experience

## Coracle Ride atParavur Backwaters
A coracle is a traditional round basket boat used by Kerala's fishing communities for centuries. Step into one of these remarkable circular vessels and experience the unique sensation of spinning and drifting as our skilled guide navigates using a single paddle. Pure joy, pure Kerala — a favourite with families and children.
What's Included
All activities are conducted with trained guides and full safety equipment. Life jackets are provided for all water activities. We prioritise your safety above all else.
